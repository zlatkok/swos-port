Shot chance table
=================

Size: 60 bytes.

For goalkeepers: kGoalieSkillTables
For players: kPlayerShotChanceTable

The table contains 30 signed 16-bit values. TeamGeneralInfo.shotChanceTable
points to one of the eight goalkeeper-skill rows while a goalkeeper is being
controlled, or to the player row otherwise. Only the fields listed below have
confirmed runtime reads; the remaining values still need analysis.

offset  length  description
------  ------  -----------
   0      2     unknown
   2      2     unknown
   4      2     unknown
   6      2     goalkeeper run speed; also halved for one approach calculation
   8      2     goalkeeper run speed used when handling a high ball
  10     32     16 goalkeeper dive-speed indices, selected with
                currentGameTick & 0x0f. The selected value is decremented and
                clamped to zero before indexing kGoalkeeperDiveDeltas.
  42      2     unknown
  44      2     unknown
  46      2     unknown
  48      2     close-ball catch chance, 0..15. The goalkeeper catches when
                ((currentGameTick & 0xf0) >> 4) is less than this value;
                otherwise the ball is deflected. Thus N means N/16 chance.
  50      2     unknown
  52      2     strong-deflection chance, 0..15
  54      2     medium-deflection chance after the strong range. This is a
                range length, not an absolute threshold. Remaining values up
                to 15 produce a weak deflection.
  56      2     unknown
  58      2     chance threshold, 0..15, for handling a ball outside the
                goalkeeper area. Two goalkeeper paths compare a random nibble
                against it before continuing their catch/approach logic.

Deflection selection
--------------------

GoalkeeperDeflectedBall derives a uniform value from 0 through 15 from bits
2..5 of currentGameTick. Values below the offset-52 field produce a strong
deflection. The following offset-54 values produce a medium deflection, and
all remaining values produce a weak deflection. In the supplied goalkeeper
rows the strong chance rises from 2/16 to 9/16 with skill, while the medium
chance remains 6/16.

Known skill progression
-----------------------

Across goalkeeper skill rows 0..7, the offset-6 speed rises from 832 to 1024,
the offset-8 speed rises from 160 to 256, and both the close-ball catch and
outside-area thresholds rise from 5 to 12. The dive-speed-index distributions
also shift toward lower indices; after the decrement performed by the code,
lower indices select lower values from kGoalkeeperDiveDeltas and therefore
require more frames to cover the same distance.
